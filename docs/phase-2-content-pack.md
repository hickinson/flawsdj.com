## 7. Biography Source

Use the biography below as the factual source. Do not invent additional claims.

FLAWS, real name Kai Hickinson, is a rhythm-led techno DJ, producer and label owner. His sound is built around punchy kicks, pressurised basslines, swinging polyrhythmic percussion, icy bleeps, levitating pads and staccato hi-hats. The result should be described as focused, physical, hypnotic and emotionally deep.

Kai was born and raised outside Peterborough, England. He grew up around his father’s record collection and was influenced by artists and sounds including Kerri Chandler, DJ Deep, Steve Bug, Roni Size and DJ EZ. He was later deeply influenced by the productions of Jeff Mills and Robert Hood.

He began producing in 2018. While at college, he discovered a practice room with CDJs and a mixer, after which DJing became a constant obsession. A college tutor encouraged him to focus on feeling over formula. This advice, combined with Kai’s belief that imperfection is essential to art, gave rise to the FLAWS moniker.

In 2019, Kai began sending tracks to DVS1. After receiving positive feedback, he travelled to Berlin and experienced his first night on a dance floor, hearing a closing set from DVS1 at Berghain. Hearing the kind of music he had been producing on such an iconic sound system was a life-changing moment.

Following this, Kai began playing gigs and connected with artists and collectives who shared his sound, including Rene Wise, MARRØN and the Eerste Communie collective in the Netherlands. Eerste Communie invited him to play several times and later made him a resident.

In 2019, Kai founded Platform 22, a label releasing music from a close-knit group of artists as well as his own debut EP, `Motion`, released in August 2019.

FLOAT Records label boss TWR72 later reached out and released three FLAWS EPs on FLOAT in 2020.

After his Eerste Communie residency chapter, his productions, release portfolio and touring schedule continued to develop, with gigs in London, Marrakech, Manchester, Berlin, New York, Detroit, Chicago and beyond.

FLAWS is currently based in the Netherlands and continues to establish his place in the international techno scene.

### Long Bio

FLAWS, the project of Kai Hickinson, is a rhythm-led techno DJ, producer and label owner whose sound is defined by clarity, pressure and movement. His sonic palette brings together punchy kicks, pressurised basslines, swinging polyrhythmic percussion, icy bleeps, levitating pads and staccato hi-hats, creating a focused but emotionally expansive form of techno.

Born and raised outside Peterborough, England, Kai grew up surrounded by his father’s record collection and absorbed the grooves of Kerri Chandler, DJ Deep and Steve Bug alongside distinctive UK sounds from Roni Size and DJ EZ. The productions of Jeff Mills and Robert Hood later opened a different world: futuristic rhythms and sound design built for clubs he had yet to experience.

Kai began producing in 2018. At college, he discovered a practice room with CDJs and a mixer, and from that point DJing became a constant obsession. A tutor recognised his dedication and encouraged him to focus on feeling over formula. That advice, combined with Kai’s belief that imperfection is essential to art, gave rise to the FLAWS moniker.

2019 became a pivotal year. Kai began sending tracks to DVS1 and, after receiving positive feedback, travelled to Berlin for his first night on a dance floor, hearing a closing set from the Berghain resident at the legendary club. Hearing music connected to his own productions on such an iconic sound system became a life-changing moment.

Encouraged by this, FLAWS began playing gigs and building relationships with others who shared his sound, including Rene Wise, MARRØN and the rising Eerste Communie collective in the Netherlands. Eerste Communie invited him to play several times and later made him a resident.

That same year, Kai founded Platform 22, a label releasing music from a close-knit group of artists alongside his own debut EP, `Motion`, released in August 2019. FLOAT Records label boss TWR72 later took notice, going on to release three FLAWS EPs on FLOAT in 2020.

With his Eerste Communie residency chapter complete, FLAWS continued to develop his productions, release portfolio and touring schedule. Gigs followed in London, Marrakech, Manchester, Berlin, New York, Detroit, Chicago and beyond. Now based in the Netherlands, FLAWS continues to expand his artistry and establish his place in the international techno scene.
